About Us

Home  ›  About

Trusted for over 60 years, H.E. Bennett Securities, Inc has been contributing to the growth of the Philippine economy by creating wealth and value. Through the years the company has shown its unwavering commitment, perseverance and dedication to its valued clients.

H. E. Bennett Securities, Inc., a proud member of the Philippines Stock Exchange (PSE), is licensed with the Securities Exchange Commission (SEC) as a Broker and Dealer. As a Trading Participant, the company conducts business in buying and selling issues of companies listed in the PSE. We provide services to individuals and institutions.

◷

History

Pioneer stock broker Henry E. Bennett started the business in the 1930’s. Briefly interrupted by World War II, he continued trading after the liberation. As Member of the Manila Stock Exchange, he incorporated the company on May 10, 1956. After Mr. Bennett demise, his wife Mrs. Mary Catherine Bennett took over the company. Today, H.E. BENNETT SECURITIES, INC. continues to serve the investing public as Trading Participant of the Philippine Stock Exchange.

Now on its 3rd generation, equipped with decades of experience, determination and perseverance, and with the aid of new tools and technology, the company is ready to face the challenges of a new era.

◎

Mission

H.E. BENNETT SECURITIES, INC., is determined to be recognized as a pioneer in the Philippine Stock Brokerage industry. We are committed to provide our Clients with the best possible service, wherever they might be, and help them fulfill their aspirations and investment goals.

◉

Our Vision

To become a globally competitive organization that provides trading solutions and deliver investment opportunities that will enable clients to manage wealth and benefit prosperously.
